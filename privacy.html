Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Overview

ADVERUNE's website is a static landing page. It does not use accounts, cookies for tracking, analytics by default, or a server-side database. The inquiry form does not send or store your data anywhere by itself — it only prepares a WhatsApp message or e-mail that you send from your own account.

Information you choose to send

When you contact us through WhatsApp or e-mail, you share the details you typed into the form (such as your name, business, and project description) with us directly through those services. Please also review the privacy policies of WhatsApp and your e-mail provider.

Analytics

No analytics is enabled by default. If analytics is added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected.

External fonts

This site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts. When the font loads, Google receives your IP address and browser information as part of that request.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the latest update.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact section once agency contact details are configured there.
